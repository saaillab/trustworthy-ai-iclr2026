Unifying Perspectives on Learning Biases: A Data-Centric Intervention for Holistic Fairness, Robustness, and Generalization

Patrick Vincent Ndowo 1 , Innocent Nyalala 1,2
1School of Engineering and Science, Indian Institute of Technology Madras Zanzibar, Tanzania · 2Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSDSAI), Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
ICLR 2026 Workshop on Principled Design for Trustworthy AI: Interpretability, Robustness, and Safety across Modalities, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (Apr 2026)
Paper Code BibTeX

TL;DR: Accepted paper at the ICLR 2026 Trustworthy AI Workshop unifying fairness, robustness, and out-of-distribution generalization via data-centric bias intervention.

Abstract

Fairness, robustness, and out-of-distribution generalization are typically studied in isolation despite sharing common roots in dataset acquisition bias, spurious shortcuts, and representational imbalance. We propose a unified data-centric framework that diagnoses and intervenes on learning biases prior to model fitting, demonstrating simultaneous gains across fairness disparities, environmental perturbations, and cross-domain transfer in resource-constrained deployments.
